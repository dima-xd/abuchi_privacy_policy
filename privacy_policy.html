Privacy Policy - Abuchi

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Abuchi ("the App") is a flashcard application for learning Chinese. It works without an account, keeps your cards on your device, and only sends data off the device in the limited cases described below. This Privacy Policy explains what the App handles, why, and which third parties are involved.

This policy applies to the Abuchi app on Android (Google Play, RuStore) and on iOS (App Store).

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created by you and stays on your device:

  • Flashcard content - cards and decks you create, including text, images, and audio you attach, stored in a local database inside the App's sandbox
  • Study statistics - progress, streaks, mastery scores, and session history
  • App settings - language, theme, training preferences, and reminder times, stored in local app preferences (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS)
  • Anonymous install ID - a random value generated once on first launch. It is not derived from your device, account, or any personal data, and it is only used for the shared-deck moderation described in section 3

This data is removed when you uninstall the App. Local backups you export yourself (a file or a Google Drive backup) are controlled by you.

2. Permissions the App Requests

Permission Why it is needed
Microphone Pronouncing practice. Speech recognition runs entirely on-device with an offline engine (Vosk); tone analysis is also on-device. Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.
Photo library Adding an image to a flashcard. The image you pick is copied into your local database. It leaves the device only if you publish the deck (section 3).
Notifications (Android) Local study reminders. They are scheduled on your device; the App has no push-notification server.
Advertising identifier (iOS) The App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to it. Ads are contextual: chosen from the request itself, not from your activity in other apps or on websites. See section 6.

Speech synthesis (text-to-speech) also runs on your device, either through the system voice or through models downloaded from the sherpa-onnx project. No card text is sent to a text-to-speech service.

3. Shared Decks You Publish

The App includes a public catalog of decks shared by users. Publishing is optional: it starts only when you choose to publish a deck, and every submission is reviewed by a moderator before it appears in the catalog.

When you publish a deck, the App uploads to our hosting provider (Supabase - database and file storage):

  • the deck contents - card text, and any images or audio attached to the cards
  • the deck title, description, language, and tags
  • up to five text-only sample cards used as a preview in the catalog
  • your anonymous install ID (see section 1), used only for rate limiting and to block devices that abuse the catalog
Published decks are public. Please do not publish personal information (your own or anyone else's) in deck titles, descriptions, or cards.

To report a deck in the catalog, use the Report action on its page: it opens an email to io.abuchi@gmail.com with the deck title and its catalog id already filled in, so we can find the entry. To have a deck you published removed, email the same address - we will delete the catalog entry and the stored file. Downloading a shared deck only fetches data; it does not send us anything beyond a download counter and the queries described in section 5.

4. Google Drive Backup (Optional)

You may optionally sign in with your Google account to back up your data to your own Google Drive. The App requests only the drive.file scope, so it can see only the files it created itself. The backup is stored only in your personal Drive; your email address is used on-device to show which account is signed in and is not transmitted to our servers. You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account settings.

Auto-backup is off unless you enable it, and it only uploads to your own Drive.

5. Third Parties and Advertising

The App uses third-party advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting SDKs, and a hosting provider for shared decks. These services receive data from your device as described below. All transmission is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

App interactions (in-app activity, app version, language, training and streak statistics) Analytics Google Firebase Analytics
Approximate location (derived from IP address at the city level) Analytics, ad delivery Google Firebase, Yandex Mobile Ads

Performance data (startup time, network timings, screen rendering) Performance monitoring Google Firebase Performance

These providers process data under their own privacy policies: Yandex (yandex.com/legal/confidential), Google (policies.google.com/privacy), Supabase (supabase.com/privacy), and the Wikimedia Foundation (foundation.wikimedia.org).

We do not sell your data and we do not use your flashcards or study content for advertising.

If you have the optional Abuchi Translate app installed on Android and enable the external translator, the text you send for translation is passed to that app. It processes text on-device and has its own privacy policy.

6. Your Choices

  • Ads. On iOS the App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier is not used to track you and ads are contextual. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Notifications. Study reminders can be turned off in the App's settings or in the system settings.
  • Microphone and photos. Both permissions can be revoked in the system settings; the related features then stop working, and nothing else is affected.
  • Analytics and crash reports. These are used to keep the App stable and are not tied to your identity. If you want them disabled, contact us and we will explain the options for your platform.
  • Backups. Auto-backup is off by default and can be turned off at any time; you can also sign out of Google Drive in the App.

7. How Long Data Is Kept

  • On your device: until you delete the data or uninstall the App.
  • Published decks: until you ask us to remove them.
  • Analytics: retained according to the provider's settings (Firebase Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months by default).
  • Crash reports: 90 days by default in Crashlytics.
  • Moderation records (anonymous install IDs of blocked devices): kept while needed to prevent abuse.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Since the App has no accounts, most data is already only on your device; for everything else, contact us at io.abuchi@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

Data sent to third parties is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS. Your local database sits in the App's private sandbox and is protected by the operating system, including device encryption when enabled. Published decks are stored with access rules that allow reading the catalog but not editing other users' entries. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the App uses third-party advertising and analytics SDKs, please supervise children's use accordingly. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International Transfers

The providers listed above may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.

13. Contact
