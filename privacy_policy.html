Privacy Policy — Abuchi

Last updated: May 18, 2025

Abuchi ("the App") is a flashcard application for learning Chinese. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

The App does not:

  • Collect or transmit any personal information to external servers
  • Create or require user accounts
  • Use advertising or analytics SDKs
  • Share any data with third parties
  • Track your location

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data the App creates stays on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • Flashcard content — cards and folders you create, stored in a local SQLite database
  • Study statistics — progress, streaks, session history, stored locally
  • App settings — preferences stored using Android SharedPreferences

This data is removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Microphone Access

The App requests microphone permission solely to enable the pronunciation practice feature. Audio is processed entirely on-device using an offline speech-recognition engine (Vosk). No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

4. Internet Permission

The App declares the INTERNET permission to support optional AI-assisted study features that use a locally running language model. No data is sent to remote servers. The permission may also be used by the Flutter framework for local debugging purposes only.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, there is nothing to disclose under COPPA or similar regulations.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised date at the top.
