Privacy Policy — Abuchi Translate

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Abuchi Translate ("the App") is an on-device translation application that translates text, photos, and speech between 37+ languages using a local AI model. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

The App does not:

  • Collect or transmit any personal information to external servers
  • Create or require user accounts
  • Use advertising or analytics SDKs
  • Share any data with third parties
  • Track your location
  • Send the text, photos, or voice input you translate to any remote service

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The only data the App stores stays on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • App settings — selected source/target languages, theme, and app language, stored using Android SharedPreferences

Translation input and output, photos, and voice input are processed in memory and are not saved by the App. This data is removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Camera Access

The App requests camera permission solely to enable the "Translate from photo" feature. Images you capture or pick from the gallery are processed entirely on-device for text recognition and translation. Photos are not recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

4. Microphone Access

The App requests microphone permission solely to enable the voice translation feature. Speech is processed on-device by the platform's speech-recognition engine and the local AI model. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server by the App.

5. Offline Operation

The translation model is bundled inside the App and runs entirely on your device. The App does not contact any server, does not download models, and does not send your translation input, output, photos, or audio over the network.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no personal data is collected at all, there is nothing to disclose under COPPA or similar regulations.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised date at the top.
