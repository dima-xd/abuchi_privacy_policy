Privacy Policy — Abuchi Translate

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Abuchi Translate ("the App") is an on-device translation application that translates text, photos, and speech between multiple languages using a local AI model. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles, how it is used, and which third parties it is shared with.

1. On-Device Processing

The translation model and the text-recognition (OCR) models are bundled inside the App and run entirely on your device. The text, photos, and voice input you translate are processed in memory on your device and are not saved by the App or sent to any translation server.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data stays on your device:

  • App settings — selected source/target languages, theme, and app language, stored using Android DataStore / SharedPreferences

This data is removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Data Collected and Shared with Third Parties

The App displays advertising through the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. To serve ads, this SDK collects data from your device and transmits it to its provider. All transmission is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Data type Purpose Collected by / shared with
Device or other identifiers (advertising ID) Advertising Yandex Mobile Ads
Approximate, coarse device and diagnostic data Advertising, fraud prevention, performance Yandex Mobile Ads

Yandex processes this data under its own privacy policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential. The text, photos, and voice input you translate are never shared with Yandex or any other third party.

4. Camera Access

The App requests camera permission solely to enable the "Translate from photo" feature. Images you capture or pick from the gallery are processed entirely on-device for text recognition and translation. Photos are not recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

5. Microphone Access

The App requests microphone permission solely to enable the voice translation feature. Speech is processed on-device by the platform's speech-recognition engine and the local AI model. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server by the App.

6. Internet Access

The App uses the internet connection only to load advertising through the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. Translation itself does not require an internet connection and works fully offline.

7. Data Deletion

Local data is deleted when you uninstall the App. To request deletion of any data associated with you, contact us at dimaxdqwerty@gmail.com and we will process your request.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the App uses a third-party advertising SDK, please supervise children's use accordingly.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised date at the top.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: dimaxdqwerty@gmail.com
